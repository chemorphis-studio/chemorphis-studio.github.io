The Production Process

PHASE 01

Input and Computational Setup

You specify the reaction, target molecule, or concept. We compute the exact parameters and set up the quantum mechanical calculations required for absolute precision.

PHASE 02

Draft and Storyboard Review

We discuss trajectories, conceptual placements, and visual setups. You have full approval over the narrative flow before heavy rendering begins.

PHASE 03

Rendering and Synchronisation

Our engine produces the 3D scene or dynamic graph. Energy plots, camera movements, and electron trajectories are perfectly synchronised to the frame.

PHASE 04

Final Delivery

You receive high-bitrate MP4 assets, web-optimised .glb files, or custom vector graphs—all formatted seamlessly for your specific platform.
