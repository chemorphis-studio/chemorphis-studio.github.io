The Features

State-of-the-Art Animation

Our core features for representing organic mechanisms effectively.

Classic Curved Arrows

Lift your curved arrows off to connect atoms, lone pairs, and bonds in three dimensions, allowing students to grasp the flow of electrons.

Butter-Smooth Animation

Watch molecules move, rotate, and arrows twist as particles approach each other. Calculated through linear interpolation to seamlessly build the motion.

Transition State Imaginary Mode Animation

On a potential energy surface (PES), the transition state is defined by a saddle point with exactly one imaginary frequency.

We animate these precise vibrational modes (along with non-imaginary ones), allowing students to visually grasp the exact pathway along which bonds break and form.

Synchronised Multidimensional Tracking

Live Energy Plots
