// 05. BREATHTAKING IMAGES

Cinematic Still Renders & Cover Art.

Transform abstract structures into arresting, publication-grade artwork engineered for a wide array of educational and branding applications.

The Value

Default molecular viewers communicate data but often fail to capture attention. Whether you are launching a course, pitching a book, or building a brand, visual authority dictates perceived value. Chemorphis leverages studio-grade ray tracing, cinematic depth of field, and physically-based material rendering (PBR) to craft visuals that demand focus.

Technical Capabilities

DIMENSION
TYPICAL SOFTWARE
CHEMORPHIS STUDIO
LIGHTING
Flat, single-point ambient lighting. Structures appear washed out and dimensionally flat.
3-point studio lighting to isolate target molecular elements and convey rich physical depth.
MATERIALS
Basic, matte plastic spheres showing no refraction, reflection, or organic textures.
Physically-based rendering (PBR) materials or volumetric textures tailored to your branding.
FOCUS
Infinite sharpness across the image (zero depth), causing visual crowding.
Cinematic depth of field, pulling the viewer's eye directly to the active site or reacting bond.
ENVIRONMENT
Plain white or black flat background with no atmospheric scale.
Volumetric atmospheric fog with light rays and subtle environmental falloff.

The Applications

  • Cover images or thumbnails for videos, podcasts, and courses.
  • High-resolution figures for modern textbooks and publications.
  • High-impact website hero banners and background layouts.
  • Branded promotional graphics and corporate presentations.
